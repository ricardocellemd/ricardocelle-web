Diagnosis first

Hair loss can be caused by androgenetic alopecia, telogen effluvium (stress, postpartum, deficiencies), hormonal factors and more. That is why we start with a medical assessment: history, scalp examination and, when indicated, blood tests. Treating without a diagnosis wastes time.

Hair PRP (platelet-rich plasma)

Prepared from a small sample of your own blood and applied to the scalp. Its growth factors stimulate the follicle and help slow hair loss and improve hair thickness. It is usually planned as an initial series followed by maintenance.

Scalp mesotherapy

Micro-injections into the scalp with active ingredients chosen for your case to nourish the follicle and support treatment. It is often combined with PRP and with oral or topical medical treatment.

A plan, not a one-off session

The best results come with consistency and follow-up: progress photos, treatment adjustments and, if ever needed, an assessment for an FUE/DHI hair transplant.

Frequently asked questions

How many hair PRP sessions will I need?

It depends on the diagnosis and your response. Usually an initial phase of several spaced sessions is followed by maintenance; we define it at your assessment.

Does PRP work for androgenetic alopecia?

It can help slow hair loss and improve hair quality as part of a medical plan. It does not replace a transplant where follicles are already gone.

Can I start with an online consultation?

Yes. In the online consultation we review your case and, if indicated, you can receive an e-prescription valid at pharmacies throughout Spain.
