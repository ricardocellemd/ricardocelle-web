Frequently asked questions

Is the online prescription valid at any pharmacy?

A private e-prescription issued by a doctor licensed in Spain can be dispensed at pharmacies throughout the country using its code.

Will I always get a prescription?

No. Prescribing depends on the medical assessment; some cases need an in-person examination or tests.

Which languages can I use?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Russian and French.
