Collagen biostimulators

Injectables that make your skin produce its own collagen, improving laxity and skin quality gradually without changing your features.

Book an assessmentMeet Dr. Celle

Types

  • Poly-L-lactic acid (Sculptra, Elleva): global volume and laxity, face and body.
  • Calcium hydroxylapatite (Radiesse): jawline definition; diluted for neck, décolletage, arms or abdomen.
  • Polycaprolactone (Ellansé, Gouri) and PDLLA (AestheFill).

Protocol and price

1–3 sessions 6–8 weeks apart; results build over 3–4 months and last 18–24 months.

  • Radiesse: from €400 per syringe.
  • Sculptra / Elleva: from €500 per vial.
  • Buttocks with Sculptra/Elleva X (2 vials): from €1,100.

Final prices including VAT.

Frequently asked questions

Biostimulator or hyaluronic acid?

Hyaluronic acid fills immediately; biostimulators rebuild your own collagen gradually. They are often combined.

How much do biostimulators cost in Barcelona?

From €400 per syringe of Radiesse and from €500 per vial of Sculptra, including VAT.
