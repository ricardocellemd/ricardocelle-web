What is it used for?

Botulinum toxin temporarily and locally relaxes the muscles that cause dynamic wrinkles. It is a prescription medicine that should only be administered by a doctor after an assessment.

  • Forehead — horizontal lines.
  • Frown lines — between the eyebrows.
  • Crow’s feet — lines at the corners of the eyes.
  • Other medical indications assessed individually, such as hyperhidrosis.

Natural results

The aim is not a frozen face but a rested one. We study your muscles in motion and adjust points and dosage so you keep your expression.

The session

  1. Medical assessment and reference photos.
  2. Treatment with micro-injections, taking just a few minutes.
  3. Review after two weeks to assess the result and touch up if needed.

Frequently asked questions

When will I see the effect?

It usually starts to show after 3–5 days, and the full effect is assessed at around 2 weeks.

How long does it last?

Usually 4 to 6 months, depending on the area, the dose and the individual.

Can I go back to my routine?

Yes, in most cases straight away. We give you instructions for the first hours, such as avoiding lying down or intense exercise.

Who should not be treated?

Among others, pregnant or breastfeeding women and people with certain neuromuscular conditions. That is why a prior medical assessment is essential.
