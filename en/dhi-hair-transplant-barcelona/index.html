DHI hair transplant, no shave

DHI implants each follicular unit with a Choi-type implanter, without opening channels first. It gives precise control at the hairline and, in many cases, avoids shaving the whole head.

FUE, DHI or Density HD

  • FUE: channels are opened first and grafts placed with forceps; the most versatile for large areas.
  • DHI: direct implantation with an implanter; ideal for the hairline, densifying between existing hair and unshaven cases.
  • Density HD: FUE for large areas plus DHI at the hairline and transition zones.

We choose the technique in your hair transplant assessment, based on your Norwood/Ludwig grade, donor area and expectations.

Unshaven hair transplant

For moderate areas, DHI allows implanting between your existing hair without shaving the recipient area — ideal if you cannot take time off or want discretion. Not every case is suitable; we assess it in consultation.

Recovery timeline

  • Days 1–10: small scabs that come off with washing.
  • Weeks 2–8: shedding of transplanted hair (normal).
  • Months 3–4: new growth starts.
  • Months 9–12: final result.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between FUE and DHI?

Both extract follicular units one by one. In FUE channels are opened first; in DHI grafts are implanted directly with an implanter, with more control of angle and depth.

Can I have a hair transplant without shaving?

Often yes for moderate areas, using DHI. It is assessed in consultation.

How much does a DHI hair transplant cost in Barcelona?

FUE up to 2,000 grafts starts at €4,400; DHI adds 15% in the Esencial and Premium plans.
