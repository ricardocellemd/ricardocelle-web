Hair PRP in Barcelona

Platelet-rich plasma is one of the best-supported medical treatments to slow hair loss and improve hair thickness. We use it after a proper diagnosis, as part of a plan with photo follow-up.

What is hair PRP?

PRP is obtained from a small sample of your own blood, centrifuged to concentrate the platelets. The plasma is applied with micro-injections where the hair is thinning. Platelets release growth factors that stimulate the follicle, extend the growth phase and improve hair thickness. Because it comes from your own blood, the risk of allergy is minimal.

Who is it for?

  • Androgenetic alopecia in men and women, especially in early or intermediate stages, while follicles are still active.
  • Telogen effluvium (diffuse shedding after stress, pregnancy, diets or illness), alongside treatment of the cause.
  • Before and after a hair transplant, to support native hair and the grafts.

It cannot regrow hair where follicles are gone: that is when a hair transplant is the option. That is why we always start with a trichoscopy assessment.

Sessions and results

The usual protocol is 3–4 sessions one month apart, then maintenance every 4–6 months. Shedding usually decreases within weeks; density and thickness are assessed from 3–6 months with standardised photos. Each session takes 30–45 minutes and you can go back to your routine the same day. PRP works best combined with scalp mesotherapy and, when indicated, medical treatment with an e-prescription.

Frequently asked questions

Does hair PRP hurt?

The micro-injections cause brief, mild discomfort. Cold is applied and most patients tolerate it well.

How many PRP sessions do I need?

Usually 3 or 4 initial sessions one month apart, then maintenance every 4 to 6 months.

When will I see results?
