What is facial mesotherapy?

Superficial micro-injections of active ingredients such as non-cross-linked hyaluronic acid, vitamins, amino acids or polynucleotides (PDRN), chosen for your skin. The aim is to improve hydration, radiance, texture and firmness.

Areas we treat

  • Face — dull, dehydrated skin or fine lines.
  • Neck and décolletage — thin skin losing firmness.
  • Hands — dehydrated skin with signs of ageing.

Natural results

Dr. Celle’s philosophy is to rejuvenate without changing who you are. Mesotherapy can be combined with other treatments (botulinum toxin, hyaluronic acid, collagen stimulators or facial PRP) in a personal plan after a medical assessment.

Frequently asked questions

How many sessions are recommended?

Usually a course of several sessions followed by maintenance. The exact number depends on your skin and the product chosen.

Is there downtime?

Small bumps or redness may appear and usually settle within hours or a few days. Most people return to their routine straight away.

Is it the same as a filler?

No. Mesotherapy improves skin quality; cross-linked hyaluronic acid fillers add volume or definition. We explain what you need at your assessment.
