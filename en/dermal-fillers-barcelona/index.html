What is hyaluronic acid?

A substance naturally present in the skin. Cross-linked hyaluronic acid fillers restore volume, define contours and deeply hydrate. They are reabsorbable and, if needed, can be corrected with hyaluronidase.

Areas we treat

  • Lips — hydration, contour definition or subtle volume.
  • Non-surgical rhinoplasty — correcting small irregularities of the nose without surgery.
  • Tear troughs — under-eye hollows.
  • Cheeks, chin and jawline — harmonisation and projection.
  • Folds — nasolabial and marionette lines.

Safety and correction

We use premium, traceable products. And if you had a poor result elsewhere, we assess correction with hyaluronidase as part of our facial emergencies and second-opinion service.

Frequently asked questions

How long do fillers last?

It depends on the area and product: roughly 6 to 18 months.

Will people notice?

Our goal is for people to notice you look well, not that you had a treatment. We work with prudent volumes and natural proportions.

Can it be reversed?

Yes. Hyaluronic acid can be dissolved with hyaluronidase if a result needs correcting.

Is there swelling afterwards?

There may be swelling or small bruises for the first few days, especially on the lips. We give you guidance to minimise them.
